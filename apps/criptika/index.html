What it is

Every day, a hidden word.

A cryptic clue joins a definition and a piece of wordplay that lead to the same answer. Criptika gives you one free cryptic every day and three with Premium. You type the answer letter by letter, ask for hints when you need them, and the clue is explained once it's solved.

There are no accounts and no ads. Progress, stats and streaks stay on the device.

My role

Criptika is my own product. I came up with the idea, designed the whole experience, from the game rules, hints and Free and Premium plans to the interface and visual identity, and I own the app.

Hints

Four kinds of help, each with a daily limit.

Three hints highlight parts of the clue; the fourth reveals a letter. Free includes one of each per day, Premium two.

01

Definición

Highlights the part of the clue that defines the answer.

02

Indicador

Highlights the word that signals the wordplay.

03

Material

Highlights the letters or words the answer is built from.

04

Revelar letra

Reveals one letter of the answer.

Solutions, streaks and library

Once a clue is solved, the app explains step by step how the answer is built. The library keeps past cryptics to look back on: the last seven days on Free, all of them with Premium.

Stats are worked out from saved progress: cryptics solved, accuracy, days played, average time, hints used and the last seven days. A streak counts consecutive days, Madrid time, with at least one cryptic solved.
